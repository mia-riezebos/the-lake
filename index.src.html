    How the model works
    • Fish regrow fastest at half capacity, 40% a year at best, with good years and bad years drawn at random. Below 150 fish, spawning starts to fail, so a thin lake crashes instead of gliding down.
    • With no catch limit, villages add effort while fishing still pays. That drives the lake down to where it just breaks even: 200 fish, a fifth of capacity.
    • With a limit, each village decides every season whether to take extra. Greed pushes one way; trust, the odds of being caught, and the size of the next penalty push back.
    • Trust drifts toward a level set by who made the rules: higher when the villages wrote them together. Seeing neighbours caught lowers it, and so does noticing the lake thin while you played fair. Mediation softens both and speeds repair.
    • A fixed limit is 90 fish a year. An adaptive one follows the expected regrowth and leans toward half capacity.
    • Every number here is a choice I made, not a measurement. The whole model is about 200 lines of JavaScript inside this page. Read it, change it, argue with it.
